## Brand & Style

This design system channels the quiet confidence of East Asian paper craft and modern literary journals. Drawing from the tactile heritage of handmade washi, sumi-e brush ink, and high-end editorial layouts (such as modern literary reviews and Notion-style digital workspaces), it provides an immersive space for language mastery, writing, and deep study.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Tactile Paper & Ink (纸墨之美):** Interfaces behave like natural parchment rather than cold backlit screens. Elements rest upon textured, warm off-white tones with deep charcoal and sumi-ink typography.
- **Editorial Zen (禅意留白):** Expansive, considered whitespace (`ma` or 間) gives vocabulary, quotes, and structural syntax room to breathe.
- **Craft & Curation:** Vermilion stamp seals (hanko/yinzhang) inspire intentional focus marks, annotations, and primary CTAs, balanced by soft, steeped-tea bamboo accents for completion and harmony.
- **Minimalist Structure:** Delicate, warm hairline dividers emulate deckle paper edges and folded book folios rather than heavy digital card borders.

## Colors

The color palette grounds the digital screen in physical textures: aged paper fiber, stone seal ink, and quiet bamboo gardens.

### Palette Architecture
- **Surface Canvas (Parchment & Silk):**
  - Base Background: `#FBF9F5` (warm ivory paper)
  - Card & Block Layer: `#F5F1E8` (pressed pulp parchment)
  - Elevated/Inset Surface: `#EDE8DF` (weathered fiber tint)
- **Primary Ink (Seal Vermilion - `#B91C1C`):** Used for focal action points, study milestones, active grammatical highlights, and authentic calligraphic punctuation.
- **Secondary Ink (Bamboo Tea - `#3F6212`):** Used for mastery confirmations, correct grammar validations, phonetic accents, and calm progress indicators.
- **Tertiary Ochre (Raw Linen - `#8C7B65`):** Subtle literary metadata, chapter tags, translation captions, and muted section markers.
- **Neutral Ink (Deep Sumi - `#1E2022`):** The primary text color; replaces pure harsh black with dense, lampblack ink. Secondary body and supportive text drop to `#4B4F54`.
- **Border & Deckle Lines:** Rendered in `#E3DDD2` (0.8px to 1px) to convey crisp, bookbinding creases.

## Typography

Typography balances evocative literary elegance with pragmatic digital legibility.

- **Newsreader** handles display headlines, bilingual literature excerpts, idiomatic annotations, pull-quotes, and formal chapter numbering. It carries distinct italic styles that enrich foreign language glossaries and pronunciation passages.
- **Inter** anchors functional interface operations: navigation hierarchies, interactive controls, inputs, data tags, and secondary explanations. Its neutral clarity ensures effortless legibility alongside expressive literary serifs.
- **Editorial Rhythm:** Extended line heights in `body-lg` (1.6x) and `headline-xl` encourage deliberate, distraction-free reading, reminiscent of carefully typeset bound books.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model honors classical book page margins, emphasizing generous outer borders and controlled column widths.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (1200px+):** Centered reader-first canvas capped at 1120px for dashboards and 740px for single-column study/reading streams. Uses a 12-column layout with 24px gutters and generous 48px outer margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 24px gutters and 32px canvas margins. Sidebars collapse into parchment drawer panels.
- **Mobile (<768px):** Single-column layout with 16px gutters and 20px margins, maintaining proportional vertical breath around editorial blocks.

### Spacing Philosophy
Whitespace is treated as an active graphic element rather than empty space. Section separations leverage `space-2xl` (64px) to preserve tranquility between distinct language units and reading passages.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids modern digital drop shadows, synthetic neon glows, and glassy blurs. Depth is tactile, physical, and paper-derived.

### Tonal Stratification
- **Ground Floor:** `#FBF9F5` serves as the overall writing desk.
- **Page Elevation:** Cards, study modules, and vocab tiles sit on `#F5F1E8`, defined by a crisp 1px fiber border (`#E3DDD2`) rather than blurred drop shadows.
- **Pressed Insets:** Active entry areas, input boxes, and quote callouts shift to `#EDE8DF`, providing an engraved, letterpress sensation.

### Tactile Edge & Lift
- **Floating Modals & Flyouts:** Grounded with a subtle, diffuse warm shadow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(44, 48, 46, 0.06), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(44, 48, 46, 0.04)`) overlaid against a solid paper outline.
- **Deckle Divider Rule:** Horizontal thematic breaks use fine 1px strokes in `#E3DDD2` or centered decorative typographic markers (`§` or `❖`).

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a soft, bookish geometry. Sharp corners feel overly utilitarian, while oversized rounded corners look too playful. 

A subtle 4px (`roundedness: 1`) radius reproduces the softened, natural corners of trimmed paper folios, stationery cards, and bound notebooks. Minor exceptions are made for pill-shaped seal badges and status tags (`rounded-full`) to simulate traditional oval and circular ink stamps.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Vermilion Seal):** Solid `#B91C1C` background with ivory `#FBF9F5` typography. Minimal hover transition to `#991B1B`. Features an optional discreet letterpress inner highlight.
- **Secondary (Tea Leaf):** Subtle `#3F6212` text and border (1px solid `#3F6212`) over transparent or `#F5F1E8` background. Transitions to `#F0EDE4` on hover.
- **Ghost / Literary Link:** Understated Sumi ink text (`#1E2022`) accompanied by a delicate 1px dotted underline in `#8C7B65`.

### Cards & Bookish Folios
- Built on `#F5F1E8` paper backgrounds with hairline borders (`#E3DDD2`).
- Card headers pair an italicized serif label (`Newsreader`) with an Inter category tag.
- Ample inner padding (`space-lg` / 24px) gives flashcard definitions and grammatical syntax examples uncrowded visual space.

### Chips & Seal Tags
- Rendered with `rounded-full` borders.
- Grammar class tags (e.g., *Noun*, *Verb*, *Idiom*) use muted paper tones (`#EDE8DF`) with `#4B4F54` text.
- Verified mastery badges use steeped tea tones (`#3F6212` with a 10% `#3F6212` tinted wash).

### Input Fields
- Styled after ruled manuscript paper: clean flat background (`#F5F1E8`), soft bottom border or 1px hairline perimeter in `#D6CEBF`.
- Focused state transitions the border to Sumi ink (`#1E2022`) or a discreet vermilion underline, dispensing with loud blue glow rings.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Checkboxes: 18x18px squares with subtle 2px rounded corners. Active checks are filled with Vermilion (`#B91C1C`) and display a crisp white checkmark resembling a quick brush flick.
- Radio buttons: Double concentric circles resembling circular book index markers.

### Literary Quote & Callout Blocks
- Left-bordered with a 3px solid vermilion or bamboo rule, tinted with `#EDE8DF` background.
- Employs `body-lg` in `Newsreader` italic, creating an authentic literary anthology experience for contextual vocabulary immersion.
